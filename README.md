## What I found interesting

What I found most interesting about this article by Joseph Barron is how it frames AI not as something that *replaces* software developers, but as the **next major abstraction** in the long history of programming. Just like compilers, higher-level languages, and frameworks changed how developers work, AI tools are shifting the focus away from manually writing every line of code and toward **problem-solving, system design, and understanding business context**.
